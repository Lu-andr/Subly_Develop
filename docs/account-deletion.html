Delete your Subly account and data

**Release draft: replace [OWNER_NAME] and [SUPPORT_EMAIL] before publishing.**

Subly is operated by [OWNER_NAME].

In the app

1. Sign in to Subly.

2. Open Profile.

3. Tap Delete Account & Data.

4. Confirm the permanent deletion. If you see an error, the request has not been confirmed: retry or contact support.

Without installing or opening the app

Email **[SUPPORT_EMAIL]** from the email address registered to your Subly account. Use the subject **Delete my Subly account**. State that you request deletion of the account and its records. Do not include your password or bank details.

The operator verifies ownership and processes the request. Before release, the operator must confirm that this support mailbox is monitored and establish a response period. A message from an unrelated email address is not sufficient proof of ownership.

What is removed

The authentication account, user profile, saved subscription/trial records, notes, and notification preferences are removed from the active database. In-app deletion also clears scheduled reminders on the device making the request. Other offline devices may retain scheduled local alerts until they reconnect/run the app, or you disable notifications/uninstall it.

Supabase backups and operational logs may have a separate configured retention period. The operator must publish that period before release.

Deleting Subly data does not cancel subscriptions with Netflix, Spotify, or other providers. Cancel those directly with each provider.
