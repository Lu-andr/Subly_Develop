# Google Play listing draft (English)

App name: Subly

Short description:
Track subscription trials and get reminders before your entered end dates.

Full description:
Subly helps you keep subscription trials and upcoming charges organized.

Add a service, enter your trial dates, price and currency, and save a cancellation link or notes. Review active trials, see which entered dates are approaching, and keep a history of records you have marked cancelled or that have expired.

Choose local reminders for 7 days, 3 days, 1 day before the entered end date, and the final day. Notification delivery depends on device permissions and operating-system settings.

Choose Russian rubles, Kazakhstani tenge, Uzbekistani som, Vietnamese dong, Philippine pesos or South African rand. South African rand is selected by default. Summaries keep currencies separate and do not convert exchange rates.

Subly does not cancel subscriptions or verify charges with service providers. You must confirm dates, prices and cancellation directly with the provider. One entered end date is tracked per record; later renewals are not automatically generated.

An account and an internet connection are needed to store and load your records. You can request account deletion from the Profile screen or through the published account-deletion page.

Release metadata to supply: developer name, support email, public privacy-policy URL, account-deletion URL, content-rating questionnaire, target audience and real device screenshots.
